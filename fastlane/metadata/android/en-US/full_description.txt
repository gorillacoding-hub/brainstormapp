Filament is a visual brainstorming canvas for organizing ideas and
connecting them into clear diagrams. Everything is stored locally
on your device.

FEATURES

• Four board templates: Free, Boxes, Circles, and Tree.
• Create, move, connect, and organize ideas on a flexible canvas.
• Multiple boards with automatic saving.
• Undo and redo.
• Ten colors for boxes and circles.
• Customize arrow colors, including blended colors where arrows
  meet.
• Choose 2D or 3D arrows and customize the background.
• Free mode or Logic mode with grid alignment and optional guide
  lines.
• Export boards as vector SVG or high-definition JPG images.
• Full view mode to give your canvas more screen space.

Filament keeps your brainstorming boards on your device.
